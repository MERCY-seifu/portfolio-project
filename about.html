Our story

Made for long conversations and slow mornings.

Sheba Coffee & Bakery began with a simple idea: celebrate the Ethiopian coffee tradition and pair every cup with a little something freshly baked, all in the welcoming rhythm of Addis Ababa.

Who we are

Neighborhood coffee with heart

We believe a good café should feel welcoming from the first hello to the last sip. Our team is committed to creating a room where neighbors, friends, and travelers can pause, connect, and enjoy comforting food.

What we serve

Fresh, simple, and full of flavor

From handcrafted espresso drinks and Ethiopian coffee to breakfast plates and lunch favorites, we keep our menu grounded in quality ingredients and familiar comfort.
